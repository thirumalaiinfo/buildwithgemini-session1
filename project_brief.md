# My agent: scriptureseedsZerah Concierge

One-liner: A conversational agent that helps parents and crafters discover children's Christian storybooks (ages 0-5) and custom leathercraft DIY kits under the scriptureseedsZerah brand.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers child's age, favorite Bible stories, preferred leather finishes, monogram initials, and past DIY project history.
- Tools: Product catalog lookup (books & leathercraft DIY kits), custom scripture/monogram personalization engine, storybook finder by age/theme.
- Catalog/UI: Rich A2UI cards displaying storybooks (with Scripture references) and leather DIY kits (with difficulty levels and materials).
- Image gen: Visual preview generator for custom leather items and storybook illustration samples.
- Sandbox: Kit pricing calculator, bundle discount engine (book + leather bookmark kit), and material requirement estimator.

Core rails (everyone): memory, tools, eval, deploy, frontend
My stretch menu (pick later): A2UI display cards, Gemini image generation, code sandbox calculations, Vertex AI RAG for scripture/crafting guides
First eval question: "I'm looking for a bedtime storybook for my 3-year-old about kindness, plus a beginner leather bookmark DIY kit for myself as a gift set under $40."
